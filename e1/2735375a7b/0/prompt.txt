setup-go v6.0.0's breaking change ("Improve toolchain handling", PR #460) calls `setGoToolchain()` unconditionally, doing `core.exportVariable('GOTOOLCHAIN', 'local')` so every later step inherits it (confirmed in `src/main.ts` on the `v7` tag). Two consequences, neither breaking today but both new with this diff:

The day upstream bumps its `go` directive past 1.27.1, that step fails hard on a commit that touched nothing, whereas under setup-go v5 (`GOTOOLCHAIN=auto`) Go would have fetched a newer toolchain transparently. Pinning staticcheck to a known version would remove the drift.
- `go.mod`'s `go 1.27.1` must now exactly match the two hard-coded `'1.27.1'` workflow pins (lines 15 and 84). A future `go.mod` bump that misses either pin fails with `go.mod requires go &gt;= X (running go 1.27.1)` instead of quietly working.
